In 2019/20, whilst at University, had the executive position of Tour Secretary in the University of Warwick Men’s Cricket Club. In this role I managed a combined £44,000 budget to organise a sports tour to South Africa. Unfortunately this trip was cancelled due to Covid-19, however I ensured that everyone on the trip got a full refund.

Whilst obtaining the refund was not particularly to do with me, this was a huge communication challenge, being the direct line of communication with the tour company and the touring group (22 university aged men). As well as this, I recognised challenges we faced, and what we could have done in the future to make our lives easier (such as obtaining group travel insurance, rather than lots of individual premiums, as well as using the university's credit card which would have given us greater financial protection).
